## 2. Mandiant M-Trends (2025-2026) Ransomware Speed, Tooling & Trusted Service Infrastructure (TSI) Recovery Denial

### A. The `22-Second` IAB Hand-Off & Top Ransomware Tooling (M-Trends 2026 p.14, 30-35)

* **Prior Compromise (`#1` Ransomware Initial Vector at `30%`)**: In M-Trends 2026, 30% of ransomware investigations began with an existing infection handed off by an Initial Access Broker (IAB). The fastest observed hand-off from initial delivery to second-stage operator execution collapsed from `>8 hours` (2022) to **`22 seconds` in 2025**: a user executed a fake browser update from **`UNC1543` (`FAKEUPDATES` / SocGholish)**, and within `22 seconds` **`UNC2165`** initiated interactive post-exploitation (`VIPERTUNNEL`) culminating in `RansomHub` deployment.
* **Top Commercial & LOTL Tools in Ransomware (M-Trends 2025 & 2026)**:
  - **Reconnaissance**: **`nltest.exe`** (`7%` [2026] / `19%` [2025]), **`SoftPerfect Network Scanner` (`NETSCAN.exe`)** (`6.5%` [2026] / `29%` [2025]), **`Advanced IP Scanner` (`advanced_ip_scanner.exe`)** (`5%` [2026] / `15%` [2025]), and **`PingCastle.exe`**.
  - **Lateral Movement & Execution**: **`PsExec.exe`** (`6.5%` [2026] / `29%` [2025]), **`PowerShell.exe`** (`5%`), **`AnyDesk.exe`** (`15%`), and **`ADAPTAGENT`** (`5%`).
  - **Credential Access & Exfiltration**: **`RAR.exe` / `WinRAR`** (`7%`), **`Rclone.exe`** (`5.5%` [2026] / `17%` [2025]), **`Mimikatz`** (`18%`), and **`EXMATTER`** (`UNC5227`).

### B. Trusted Service Infrastructure (TSI) Recovery Denial & "Second Wave" Extortion (M-Trends 2026 p.62-66)
Mandiant highlights a strategic shift in ransomware operations from simple endpoint encryption to **systematic Recovery Denial** targeting **Trusted Service Infrastructure (TSI)** (the control planes used by defenders to administer and rebuild the enterprise):

1. **Targeting the 4 Pillars of TSI**:
   - **Identity & PKI (`Active Directory` & `ADCS`)**: Abusing misconfigured certificate templates (`ESC1`/`ESC8`) to mint persistent rogue certificates and compromising `Sync_`/`MSOL_` hybrid connectors.
   - **Virtualization Consoles (`VMware vCenter` & `ESXi`)**: Pivoting via Active Directory admin groups bound to vCenter permissions to mass-kill guest VMs, mount DC snapshots offline (`Snapshot Mounting`), and encrypt `.vmdk` datastores.
   - **Backup Control Planes (`Veeam`, `Rubrik`, `Cohesity`, `Commvault`)**: Exploiting Veeam `CVE-2023-27532` (`FORGEDGRIT`) or harvesting domain-joined backup admin credentials to **unlink backup catalogs, reduce retention policies to 1 day, or re-initialize storage arrays**.
   - **Endpoint Configuration Managers (`SCCM`, `Intune`, `PDQ Deploy`, `Ansible`)**: Turning patch distribution infrastructure into simultaneous mass-encryption engines.
2. **Preventing the "Second Wave" Re-Compromise**:
   - During high-pressure recovery, organizations frequently restore VM snapshots or golden images taken *after* initial compromise that still contain dormant attacker persistence (`SystemBC`, web shells, injected OAuth secrets, or rogue local accounts).
   - **Active Resilience Mandate**: Decouple TSI authentication from production Active Directory (zero domain trust on backup and ESXi management planes), enforce out-of-band FIDO2 MFA, scan all backup images offline with YARA/CHIRP before restoration, and rotate `krbtgt` twice plus all ADCS/SAML signing keys before reconnecting network segments.
