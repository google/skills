## 1. PRC State-Sponsored APTs (Volt Typhoon, Flax Typhoon, Salt Typhoon, `UNC6201`, `UNC5221`, `UNC3886` & AI Distillation)

### A. Volt Typhoon (`AA24-038A`, `AA23-144A` / Dragos `VOLTZITE`): Critical Infrastructure Pre-Positioning & LOTL

* **Strategic Objective**: Pre-position across US critical infrastructure (Water, Energy, Communications, Transportation, Defense Industrial Base) to enable disruptive/destructive OT impacts during geopolitical crises rather than traditional data theft.
* **Core Tradecraft & MITRE ATT&CK**:
  - **Initial Access via Edge Appliances (`T1190`)**: Exploits internet-facing Fortinet FortiGuard, Ivanti Connect Secure, NETGEAR ProSAFE, Cisco RV, and Citrix NetScaler appliances.
  - **Covert ORB Proxying (`T1090.003` / FBI `KV-Botnet` FLASH)**: Routes all C2 traffic through compromised Small Office / Home Office (SOHO) routers (ASUS, Cisco RV320/RV325, DrayTek Vigor, NETGEAR, Ubiquiti) acting as Operational Relay Boxes (ORBs) so traffic originates from domestic residential/ISP IP space.
  - **Strict Living Off the Land (`T1059.001`, `T1003.003`, `T1090.001`)**:
    * Extracts Active Directory `ntds.dit` and `SYSTEM` hive using `vssadmin create shadow /for=C:` or `ntdsutil.exe "ac i ntds" "ifm" "create full C:\Windows\Temp\..."`.
    * Establishes multi-hop internal TCP relays across segmented IT/OT subnets using native Windows port forwarding: `netsh interface portproxy add v4tov4 listenaddress=<IP> listenport=<PORT> connectaddress=<TARGET_IP> connectport=<TARGET_PORT>`.
    * Enumerates environment using `wmic`, `ipconfig`, `netstat -ano`, `arp -a`, and `reg query`.

### B. Flax Typhoon (`AA26-113A` / FBI `Raptor Train` FLASH): China-Nexus Covert Networks of Compromised Devices (IoT ORBs)

* **Advisory Context**: Joint international advisory (Apr 21, 2026) and FBI FLASH alerts detailing how PRC-nexus actors build and maintain sprawling botnets (`Raptor Train`) of compromised IoT devices, IP cameras, Digital Video Recorders (DVRs), NAS devices, and End-of-Life (EOL) enterprise edge appliances (`T1583.003`, `T1584.005`, `T1090.003`).
* **Operational Mechanics**: Compromised SOHO/IoT devices maintain encrypted reverse tunnels (`Shadowsocks` on TCP/8388, reverse `SSH -R`, custom TLS, or `HiatusRAT` packet-sniffing/SOCKS5 implants on EOL DrayTek/Cisco routers) back to upstream management nodes. Inbound reconnaissance or password spraying from domestic residential/business IPs frequently originates from an unwitting victim device in an ORB mesh.

### C. Salt Typhoon (`AA25-246A` / Mandiant `UNC5807`): Telecommunications & Core Router Espionage

* **Strategic Objective & M-Trends 2026 Findings (p.66, 79)**: Deep multi-year persistence (active since at least 2020) inside Tier-1 telecommunications providers and ISP backbone routing infrastructure to intercept metadata, lawful intercept (CALEA) systems, and executive communications.
* **Core Tradecraft**:
  - Exploits Cisco IOS XE web UI vulnerabilities (`CVE-2023-20198`, `CVE-2023-20273`) and exposed edge VPN/router flaws to create local privilege-15 accounts.
  - Invokes restricted built-in **application subshells** on core network appliances and configures **SSH services on non-standard ports** for layered persistence.
  - Spawns Linux containers via `guestshell`, creates unauthorized **GRE (`ip_protocol = "GRE"` / protocol `47`)** or IPsec tunnels, and uses device-native packet capture utilities (**Embedded Packet Capture [`EPC` / `monitor capture` / `SPAN`]**) to sniff cleartext **TACACS+ (`TCP/49`)** and **RADIUS (`UDP/1812`, `1813`)** authentication traffic in transit, staging pcaps on internal file-transfer hosts before edge exfiltration.

### D. `UNC6201`, `UNC5221` & `UNC3886` (M-Trends 2024-2026): Hypervisor Implants (`BRICKSTORM`, `VIRTUALPITA`) & `393-Day` Dwell Times

* **GTIG Attribution Note (M-Trends 2026 p.66, 80)**: While public reporting has grouped `UNC6201` and `UNC5221` under "Silk Typhoon," Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) tracks `UNC6201` and `UNC5221` as distinct PRC-nexus espionage clusters.
* **`UNC6201` (`BRICKSTORM` & VM Snapshot Cloning - `393-Day` Average Dwell Time)**:
  - Sustains multi-year intrusions (**average dwell time of `393 days`**, outlasting standard 90-day and 360-day SIEM retention) by deploying **`BRICKSTORM`** (`1.2%` of all M-Trends 2026 malware), a Go-based SOCKS and WebSockets (WSS over HTTPS) implant on non-EDR Linux/BSD edge appliances, VMware vCenter servers, and ESXi hosts. Persistence mechanisms include **Apache Tomcat `AddFilter` servlet filter injection** in vCenter web apps, **`/etc/sudoers` tampering**, and **`ip6tables` / `iptables` local port redirection**.
  - Harvests Tier-0 credentials without touching guest OS EDR agents by **cloning or snapshotting running Domain Controller and PAM virtual machines in vCenter (`Snapshot Mounting`: `CreateSnapshot_Task`, `CloneVM_Task`, `ReconfigVM_Task` attaching `.vmdk` disks to an alternate guest VM)**, where **`vmtoolsd.exe` spawns archive/copy utilities (`7z.exe`, `tar.exe`, `rar.exe`, `xcopy.exe`, `robocopy.exe`)** against mounted `.vmdk` volumes to dump `ntds.dit` and `SYSTEM` hives offline.
* **`UNC5221` (`TRAILBLAZE`, `BRUSHFIRE`, `ZIPLINE` & Zero-Day VPN Chaining)**:
  - Exploited chained zero-days in Ivanti Connect Secure (`CVE-2023-46805`, `CVE-2024-21887`, and `CVE-2025-22457`), deploying **`TRAILBLAZE`** (in-memory dropper hooking the web process), **`BRUSHFIRE`** (passive implant hooking `SSL_read`), and **`BRICKSTORM`**.
* **`UNC3886` (`VIRTUALPITA`, `VIRTUALPIE`, `CASTLETAP`, `MOPSLED` - M-Trends 2024 & 2025)**:
  - Targets firewall, IoT, and virtualization layers (Fortinet, Juniper Junos, VMware ESXi/vCenter `CVE-2023-34048`, `CVE-2022-22948`).
  - Installs malicious unsigned **vSphere Installation Bundles (VIBs)** (`esxcli software vib install` with `--no-sig-check` or tampered Acceptance Levels) deploying **`VIRTUALPITA`** (ESXi VMCI socket implant listening on **`vsock://` ports `8337` or `18443`** to pivot stealthily between ESXi hypervisors and guest VMs without traversing physical network taps) and **`VIRTUALPIE`**, extracting vCenter `vpxd` PostgreSQL credentials.

### E. Industrial-Scale AI Model Distillation Campaigns (`AA26-251A`, Sep 08, 2026)

* **Advisory Context**: Joint NSA / CISA / FBI advisory warning that China-based AI companies (**DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI**) conduct systematic, industrial-scale knowledge distillation campaigns against US frontier AI models (`MITRE ATLAS AML.T0024.002`, `AML.T0040`, `AML.T0042`).
* **Tradecraft**: Orchestrates millions of synthetic prompts across distributed residential proxies and rotated cloud API keys to extract proprietary reasoning chains, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) traces, code-generation capabilities, and safety alignment boundaries.

## 2. Russian State-Sponsored APTs (FSB Center 16, Laundry Bear / APT28, Midnight Blizzard / APT29, `APT44`)

* **FSB Center 16 / Berserk Bear Router Hygiene (`AA26-194A`, Jul 08, 2026)**: Exploits legacy Cisco Smart Install (`CVE-2018-0171`, TCP port `4786`), default SNMP community strings (`public`/`private`, `CVE-2008-4128`, UDP port `161`), and TACACS+ (`TCP port 49`). Exfiltrates router `running-config` files via unencrypted **TFTP (UDP port 69)** from router IPs (`principal.ip`) to actor-controlled non-RFC1918 external IPs, cracking Type-5/Type-7 password hashes offline.
* **Laundry Bear / APT28 (`FOREST BLIZZARD` / Dragos `GRAPHITE`) Zimbra & EdgeRouter Campaigns (`AA26-204A`, M-Trends 2025)**: Targets Western governments, energy/rail logistics, and defense contractors via **Zimbra Collaboration Suite (ZCS)** zero-day **`CVE-2025-66376`** (stored XSS / SSRF in `/service/soap/` webmail rendering) combined with **Pass-the-Cookie (`ZM_AUTH_TOKEN`)** session hijacking (`T1539`, `T1550.004`) across multiple external IPs (`count_distinct($e.principal.ip) >= 3`), Outlook NTLM hash theft (`CVE-2023-23397`), and compromised Ubiquiti EdgeRouters.
* **Midnight Blizzard / SVR (`APT29` / `NOBELIUM`) Cloud & OAuth Persistence (`AA24-057A`, M-Trends 2025)**: Executes OAuth device-code phishing (`device_code`, `Consent to application`, `Add OAuth2PermissionGrant`) and low-and-slow password spraying from residential proxy networks; grants `Application.ReadWrite.All`, `RoleManagement.ReadWrite.Directory`, or `Mail.ReadWrite` (`Add app role assignment to service principal`) to OAuth Service Principals, injects credentials via `Add service principal credentials`, `Update application - Certificates and secrets management` (`AZURE_AD` / `OFFICE_365`), or `CreateServiceAccountKey` (`GCP_CLOUDAUDIT`), and mines internal SharePoint/GitHub repositories for `.pem`/`.key` secrets.
* **`APT44` (Sandworm / Dragos `ELECTRUM` & `KAMACITE` - M-Trends 2025 p.7, 43)**: Distributes trojanized software installers (`DARKCRYSTALRAT`) via torrents, targets mobile messaging apps (Signal/Telegram) on tactical devices, and conducts destructive OT/ICS wiper operations (`Industroyer2`, `AcidPour`).

## 3. DPRK Insider Threats (`UNC5267`), Web3 Supply-Chain Heists & Iranian Espionage (`UNC1549`, `APT42`, `UNC3313`)

### A. DPRK Remote IT Worker Insider Threats (`UNC5267` - M-Trends 2025 & 2026, FBI Cyber PINs/PSAs)

* **Scale & Dwell Time (M-Trends 2025 p.52-56 & M-Trends 2026 p.12, 16, 42)**: **`UNC5267`** (subordinate to the DPRK Munitions Industry Department **313 General Bureau**) was the **#1 most frequently investigated threat group in the Americas** across both M-Trends 2025 and M-Trends 2026, driving Insider Threat to `6%` of all global initial infection vectors with a **median dwell time of `122 days`** (nearly 9x the 14-day global median).
* **Laptop Farms, IP-over-KVM Hardware & Extortion Pivot**:
  - Operatives use stolen US/European identities, VoIP numbers, and in-country **facilitators** to receive corporate-provisioned laptops at residential "laptop farms."
  - Facilitators attach hardware **IP-over-KVM switches (`TinyPilot`, `PiKVM`, `JetKVM`, USB composite HID devices)** or install commercial RMM software (`AnyDesk`, `Chrome Remote Desktop`, `TeamViewer`, `RustDesk`) connected via **`Astrill VPN`** or residential proxies (brief VPN dropouts frequently leak PRC/DPRK-adjacent source IPs).
  - When terminated or exposed, `UNC5267` workers increasingly pivot to **insider data-theft extortion**, demanding ransom payments not to leak proprietary source code and internal documents.
* **DPRK Web3 & Developer Supply-Chain Clusters (M-Trends 2025 p.71-73) & FBI Kimsuky `Quishing` Alerts**:
  - **`UNC5342`**: Uses fake developer job tests on GitHub/npm/PyPI to deliver the **`BEAVERTAIL`** JavaScript/Python stealer and **`INVISIBLEFERRET`** cross-platform Python implant on macOS, Linux, and Windows developer workstations.
  - **`UNC4899` (`TraderTraitor`), `UNC4736`, `UNC1069` & `UNC3782`**: Conduct cascading software supply-chain attacks (`UNC4736`, `UNC4899`), Telegram social engineering (`UNC1069`), and Solana/TRON/Ethereum smart-contract crypto drainers (`UNC3782`). Also note **`UNC5142` (`EtherHiding`)** storing second-stage infostealer C2 payloads inside immutable BNB/Ethereum smart contracts via compromised WordPress sites.
  - **FBI Alert on Kimsuky (`APT43`) QR-Code Spearphishing (`Quishing`)**: Sends spearphishing emails with embedded QR codes that force targets to scan the link on unmanaged mobile phones, bypassing desktop EDR/proxy inspection to steal cloud SSO session tokens.

### B. Iranian Espionage, Third-Party VDI Pivots & Wiper Personas (`UNC1549`, `APT42`, `UNC3313`, `APT34`, `UNC2428`, `UNC1860`)

* **`UNC1549` (*Tortoiseshell* / IRGC Overlap - M-Trends 2025 p.59 & 2026 p.21, 67)**: Targets global aerospace, aviation, and defense sectors by compromising less-defended third-party suppliers and logging into vendor **Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI)** instances with stolen credentials; bypasses VDI process launch restrictions (`AppLocker`) to deploy **`MINIBIKE`** and **`TWOSTROKE`** implants over geolocated cloud C2.
* **`APT42`, `UNC3313` (MuddyWater), `APT34`, `UNC2428` (*Black Shadow*) & `UNC1860` (*Sacred Manticore* - M-Trends 2025 p.57-60)**:
  - **`APT42`**: Rapport-building social engineering using Google Sites/Dropbox lures and spoofed Google Meet/Workspace login pages.
  - **`UNC3313`**: Disseminates 9+ legitimate RMM installers (`SimpleHelp`, `AnyDesk`, `ScreenConnect`, `Atera`, `Syncro`) alongside the **`JELLYBEAN`** dropper and **`CANDYBOX`** implant.
  - **`APT34` & `UNC2428`**: `APT34` deploys **`DODGYLAFFA`** and **`SPAREPRIZE`**; `UNC2428` wraps malware in deceptive GUI installers (**`CACTUSPAL`** mimicking Palo Alto GlobalProtect and `RafaelConnect.exe` / **`LONEFLEET`** -> **`LEAFPILE`** -> **`MURKYTOUR`**).
  - **`UNC1860` (MOIS)**: Provides persistent access to front personas (*Homeland Justice*, *Karma*, *Handala Hack*, *Cyber Toufan*) deploying **`ROADSWEEP`**, **`COOLWIPE`**, and **`POKYBLIGHT`** wipers.
